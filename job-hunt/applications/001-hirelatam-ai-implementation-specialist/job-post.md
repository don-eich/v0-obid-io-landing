## Responsabilidades

**A) AI Implementation, Automation & Field Tooling**
- Implements, customizes, and maintains AI-powered workflows for project management, cost tracking, personnel coordination, document processing, and live operational dashboards (drilling progress, permitting statuses).
- Develops and integrates digital tools for field logistics (time tracking, purchasing, accommodation logistics, data reporting); centralizes corporate documentation across OneDrive and Google Workspace.
- Builds prompt libraries, custom AI agents, and standardized templates for investor presentations, market updates, technical summaries, and consultant trackers.
- Independently verifies all AI-generated outputs for factual accuracy, source validity, numerical precision, and consistency before distribution, documenting verification steps.

**B) Executive Support, Research & Consultant Synthesis**
- Attends meetings with executives, technical consultants, and partners; turns discussions into task trackers and AI-driven workflows.
- Maintains data rooms, documentation repositories, and trackers for deliverables, milestones, and consulting outputs.
- Produces investor decks, market intelligence updates, technical one-pagers, executive status reports.
- Synthesizes deliverables from strategy and engineering consultants; works with geologists, mining engineers, metallurgists, legal counsel, permitting specialists.

**C) US Government Funding, Appropriations & Lobbying Support**
- Researches federal funding mechanisms (grants, loans, appropriations, defense stockpile/offtake programs) for critical minerals and infrastructure.
- Maps agencies, programs, personnel, eligibility, funding brackets, timelines (DOE, DoD, DPA Title III, FAST-41, DOI/USFS).
- Prepares briefing documents, one-pagers, stakeholder lists; tracks appropriations cycles and RFPs.

**D) Talent Sourcing, Immigration Coordination & Cross-Domain Operations**
- Sources and screens technical manpower across Canada, Mexico and LATAM with recruiting partners.
- Supports visa / work authorization workflows for technical hires.
- Manages high-volume workstreams across exploration, construction, refining, and federal relations.

## Requisitos
- 3+ years hands-on deploying AI tools, workflow automations, and operational systems in a business, consulting, or technical environment.
- Advanced proficiency across AI platforms (ChatGPT, Claude, Grok or similar), automation tools, productivity suites; strict commitment to independently verifying data, citations, calculations.
- Strong organization, note-taking, analytical research and writing; distilling dense technical documents into executive briefs and decks.
- Ability to master unfamiliar technical domains quickly (mining, construction, energy processing, federal appropriations) and deliver autonomously.
- OneDrive, Google Workspace; managing competing priorities across multiple entities and stakeholders.
- English C1/C2, written and spoken.
- Full-time availability in US business hours.
- Workstation, HD webcam, noise-canceling headset, high-speed internet, quiet home office.

**Preferidos:** background en ingeniería / construcción / minería / energía / industria pesada; experiencia con grants federales de USA; low-code/no-code, dashboards BI, trackers operativos automatizados; reclutamiento internacional / inmigración USMCA.
